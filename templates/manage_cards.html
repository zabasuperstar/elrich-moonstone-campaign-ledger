Import cards from CSV

Add characters, upgrades, and campaign cards in one upload.

Download CSV template

Use one row per card and faction. Repeat the card name and URL on another row to assign it to another faction. Supported types are character, upgrade, and campaign_card. For characters, put the card page link in card_url; for upgrades and campaign cards, put the image link in image_url. URLs may be blank or use HTTPS.

type,name,faction,card_url,image_urlcharacter,Example Character,Dominion,https://example.com/character,upgrade,Example Upgrade,Shades,,https://example.com/upgrade.pngcampaign_card,Example Campaign Card,Commonwealth,,https://example.com/campaign.png

Maximum 1 MB and 1,000 non-empty rows. Existing cards are matched by type and name; import adds faction assignments and updates a URL when a non-empty URL is supplied. Inactive cards stay inactive.
